What the silver costs

It only works on dark. Silver is mid-grey once you remove the illusion of reflection. On the cream body background it collapses to grey mush — see the failing swatch above. The site is a cream-ground site, so a silver mark cannot be the primary; it can only be the dark-ground variant.
It is the system's first gradient. Every other element in Editorial Bloom is flat. One gradient in the logo and nowhere else reads as an inconsistency rather than a feature, unless dark surfaces become a deliberate second register (deck covers, merch, app splash).
It does not survive small sizes or flat print. At 24px the bands average out to flat grey — compare the third mark in each swatch. Real metal in print means a spot metallic ink or foil, priced per pass, and a flat fallback is required for the favicon regardless.
Cream on ink gets you 90% of it for free. The ink + cream swatch is already in the palette, needs no gradient, holds at 24px, and prints in one colour. On a dark ground cream reads as bright metal to most eyes.
