Black + silver — in context

Approved geometry. Silver on the left of each pair, in-palette cream on the right — so you can judge whether the gradient earns its cost at that size.

Structuring the need — what actually has to be decided

Colour is the last of these, not the first. Each slot has hard constraints; silver only satisfies some of them.

  1. Primary mark — the one used 90% of the time Lives on the cream site background. Must be flat, must hold at 24px in the header, must print in one colour. silver cannot do this
  2. Reversed mark — for dark grounds Footer, dark sections, social cards. Needs contrast against Ink at any size. Cream and silver both qualify; cream also survives shrinking. silver is a candidate
  3. Small / system mark — favicon, avatar, app icon 16–32px. No gradient survives here, so a flat cut is mandatory regardless of what you choose above. silver cannot do this
  4. Print mark — one-colour and spot Stationery, stamps, invoices. Needs a single-ink version. Silver means foil or spot metallic, quoted per pass. silver costs money here
  5. Expressive mark — large, dark, screen-only Deck covers, OG cards, splash, merch, hero moments. No size or ink constraint, so the gradient can actually be seen. silver belongs here

So the question is not "silver or blue" — it is whether slot 5 is worth having. Slots 1, 3 and 4 have to be flat no matter what you pick.
