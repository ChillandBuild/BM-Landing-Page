Round four — boundaries pushed

Four marks with sharper concepts than "flower plus dots," and four wordmarks that carry an idea, not just styling. Everything stays in the palette. Click favorites in each group.

L6 — The Morph

Read it clockwise from the top: a rigid matrix cell softens, rounds, and finally becomes a petal. The whole company story — structure becoming life — in one rotating glyph. Animates as a continuous morph.

L7 — The Sprout

A quiet grid of software cells — and one cell has germinated, growing a stem right out of the matrix with a coral bud. Warm, human, slightly witty. Unmistakably "something alive is growing in this system."

L8 — The Cutout

The flower isn't printed on the tile — it's cut out of it, petals turned 45° into an ✕: the matrix crossing. Negative space does the work. Heaviest presence of the set; instantly iconic at any size.

L9 — Plotted

A bloom plotted at the origin of a coordinate grid — petals living in the four quadrants, coral at (0,0). The most "engineering" of all: growth, measured. Feels like a scientific instrument's stamp.
