A — The Living Grid

The name IS the concept: a structured matrix of dots that blooms into something organic. Warm paper background, near-black ink, one vivid accent. The logo (a matrix cell blooming) and the site share the same generative dot motif — it can animate: dots bloom under your cursor. Nobody else looks like this.

B — Swiss Precision

Pure typographic confidence: massive tight-set type, strict visible grid, black on white, one electric blue accent. Logo becomes a sharp "BM" wordmark with an asterisk/matrix glyph. Reads instantly premium and engineering-led. Risk: the aesthetic is loved by dev-tool brands (Vercel, Linear territory).

D — Editorial Bloom

Magazine energy: cream paper, hard color blocking, oversized serif with italic emphasis, a literal abstract flower mark. Feels like a creative studio with engineering depth. Warmest and most human of the four. Risk: leans "design agency" — may undersell the enterprise/AI engineering side.

These are direction sketches, not final designs — palette, exact fonts, and the logo get refined after we pick a lane. Mixing is allowed (e.g., A's concept with C's dark mood).
